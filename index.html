About

Hi! I am Hayeong Ryu, an M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence at Chung-Ang University, where I am a research assistant in the Intelligent Information Processing Lab (IIPL), advised by Professor YoungBin Kim. I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from Seokyeong University. Here is my CV.

My research interests lie in natural language processing, trustworthy AI, and large language models. I work on making LLMs and vision-language models more faithful to what they actually know — detecting and mitigating hallucination through internal signals and decoding-time intervention, factual knowledge distillation, and evaluation methods that measure outcomes rather than surface edits.
